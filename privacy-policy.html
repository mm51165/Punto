Punto+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

Punto+ ("the app") is a padel match scoring app for Apple Watch. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

  • Player names — entered by you during match setup. Stored locally on your device only.
  • Match scores and history — game results, scores, and statistics. Stored locally on your device.
  • Apple ID name — if you choose to sign in with Apple, we use your name to pre-fill player fields and identify your stats. We do not store your Apple ID email or credentials.
  • Health data — if you enable Apple Health integration, the app logs padel matches as workouts (duration and estimated calories). This data is written to Apple Health and never leaves your device.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud Sync (Pro feature), your match history is synced via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store. This data is managed by Apple under their privacy terms. We do not operate any servers and have no access to your iCloud data.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data
  • We do not use tracking or advertising frameworks
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not store data on external servers

Subscriptions

Punto+ offers an optional annual subscription (Punto+ Pro) managed entirely through Apple's App Store. Payment and subscription management are handled by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your Apple Watch using on-device storage. If iCloud Sync is enabled, match history is additionally stored in your personal iCloud account. We do not operate any servers or databases.

Data Deletion

You can delete your match history at any time from within the app. Signing out removes your stored name. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
